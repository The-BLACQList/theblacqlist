The BLACQList is a national platform where people find, trust, and support Black-owned businesses, creatives, professionals, and events, and where every dollar spent helps make the Black economy visible.

## The problem it solves

Black-owned businesses are hard to find and easy to lose track of. The options today are scattered and weak:

- **Word of mouth** disappears the moment the conversation ends. There is no shared, searchable record.
- **Google and Yelp** were not built for this. A business shows up as a thin listing with a star rating and no story, no values, no real presence.
- **Instagram** is great for vibes but terrible for discovery. You cannot search "Black-owned hair stylist near me who takes appointments this week" and get a clean answer.
- **Old directories** feel like phone books. They are ugly, out of date, and nobody trusts them.

So people who genuinely want to support Black-owned businesses cannot find them quickly, and great businesses stay invisible. The money that wants to flow toward them gets lost.

## Why now

Three things are true at once. The intent to support Black-owned businesses is real and growing. The tools to build a polished national platform are finally cheap and fast. And no one has built the trusted, beautiful, national home for this yet. The BLACQList is moving into that gap.

## The product: BLACQList Pages

This is the heart of it, so know it cold.

Every business, creative, professional, and event gets a **BLACQList Page**. A Page is not a directory listing. It is a micro-website. The promise is that every Page looks meaningfully better than a Google Business Profile or a Yelp listing on every level.

A Page includes:

- A strong hero with a cover image, the name, a tagline, and a clear action button like Book, Order, Call, or Visit
- The basics at a glance: hours, open or closed right now, phone, address, website, and socials
- A real story: who they are, where they came from, what they stand for
- What they offer: services, a portfolio gallery, event details, or products
- A media gallery so the place actually looks like something
- A trust badge that grows from Unclaimed to Claimed to Verified to Certified
- A way for the community to save, share, and support the business

The action button is always visible. The Page never looks empty or broken. It is built to make a small business look as good online as a company that paid thousands for a website. That is the pitch to every business owner: a presence that beats a five hundred dollar website, set up in about twenty minutes.

## How it makes money

The BLACQList does not charge people to find businesses. Discovery stays free. Money comes in over time as the platform grows, in this order:

1. **Free listings first.** Get the platform full and useful. No revenue yet, on purpose. A full, trusted platform is what everything else is built on.
2. **Paid tiers and featured placement.** Businesses can upgrade for better analytics, featured spots, and sponsored placement. This is the first real revenue.
3. **Job and event promotion fees.** Posting is free at a basic level. Featured and extended promotion costs money.
4. **Marketplace fees.** Later, vendors sell products directly on the platform and The BLACQList takes a small share of each sale.
5. **The dollar-flow map.** Long term, the platform can show a community how much money is circulating through Black-owned businesses. That civic value becomes its own draw and its own moat.

The simple version to say out loud: it is free to use, businesses can pay to stand out, and later you can buy directly on it.

## Quick glossary

- **Page:** A business or person's full profile on The BLACQList. A micro-website, not a directory line.
- **Listing:** Any entry on the platform. We need a strong base of them at launch.
- **Seed listings:** The first set of quality listings we put on before launch so the platform feels full and useful on day one.
- **Claim:** When a business owner takes control of their Page to manage it themselves.
- **Trust tiers:** The badge levels a Page can earn, from Unclaimed to Claimed to Verified to Certified. Higher trust means more credibility.
- **Marketplace:** The later stage where vendors sell products directly on the platform.
- **GMV:** Gross merchandise value, meaning the total dollar value of sales that flow through the marketplace.
- **Dollar-flow map:** The long-term feature that shows how Black dollars circulate through a community.
