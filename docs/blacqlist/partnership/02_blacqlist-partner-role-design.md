# Partner Role & Ramp: Ebony's Lanes

This is where your strengths meet the work. The BLACQList needs three things from a partner more than anything: someone who can get real businesses onto the platform, someone who can build the community around it, and someone who can keep the whole effort organized. That is logistics, presence, and communication. That is you.

---

## Your mandate in one line

**You own the field and the people. Chalece owns the product and the build. Together you make the launch land.**

You are not support staff. You are a partner with real territory. The platform's success in Atlanta runs through you.

---

## The four lanes

You will touch all four. Lane 1 is where you lead, because it is what launch needs most. The others grow from it.

### Lane 1: Biz dev and listing acquisition

**What you own:** Getting great Atlanta businesses onto The BLACQList. You are the reason the platform is full and alive at launch.

**Why you:** This is relationship work. It is walking into a shop, looking the owner in the eye, and making them feel seen. Your in-person presence and the way you communicate are the whole game here. People trust you in a room.

**What it looks like:**
- [ ] Build a running list of Black-owned Atlanta businesses worth featuring
- [ ] Visit, call, and message owners to get them excited about a Page
- [ ] Help owners claim or create their Page, or gather what we need to build it for them
- [ ] Capture the basics on the spot: name, hours, contact, a photo or two, their story

**Goal it advances:** The 150 or more Atlanta listings the launch needs. This is the number that makes or breaks day one.

**How we measure it:** Businesses contacted, businesses signed on, and listings live with a photo and a story.

### Lane 2: Community and partnerships

**What you own:** The relationships that make The BLACQList part of Atlanta, not just an app. Civic groups, Black business associations, markets, pop-ups, and events.

**Why you:** You already move through Atlanta with a network and a platform. You speak well in front of people. You can stand up at an event and make a room care about this.

**What it looks like:**
- [ ] Identify the chambers, associations, and community groups worth a partnership
- [ ] Represent The BLACQList at markets, pop-ups, and events
- [ ] Turn your existing audience into early supporters and early listings
- [ ] Bring back warm introductions that feed Lane 1

**Goal it advances:** Community-powered growth, which is how the platform expands without paying for every customer.

**How we measure it:** Partnerships opened, events represented, and warm introductions delivered.

### Lane 3: Operations and logistics

**What you own:** Keeping the seeding effort organized so nothing falls through the cracks. The pipeline from "business we want" to "Page that is live and good."

**Why you:** You have a brain for logistics. You can run a process, track a list, and keep many moving pieces straight without losing the thread.

**What it looks like:**
- [ ] Maintain the master tracker of businesses: contacted, interested, signed, live
- [ ] Run a simple quality check so every Page has the basics before it goes live
- [ ] Coordinate the logistics of events and outreach pushes
- [ ] Flag bottlenecks early so we fix them before launch

**Goal it advances:** Listing quality and throughput. A full platform is good. A full platform of great Pages is what wins.

**How we measure it:** Pipeline moving on schedule and the share of live listings that meet the quality bar.

### Lane 4: Brand and spokesperson

**What you own:** Being a face and voice of The BLACQList in person and on camera.

**Why you:** You already build an audience and speak with confidence. You know how to show up real and make people pay attention without being fake about it.

**What it looks like:**
- [ ] Speak for The BLACQList at events and on camera when it fits
- [ ] Lend your audience to the launch where it makes sense and feels natural
- [ ] Keep the brand voice consistent: clear, proud, useful, never overhyped

**Goal it advances:** Awareness and trust, which make every other lane easier.

**How we measure it:** Appearances, reach, and the warmth of the response.

---

## How the lanes interlock

They feed each other in a loop. Lane 2 community work surfaces warm leads. Lane 1 turns those leads into listings. Lane 3 keeps the pipeline clean so the listings are good. Lane 4 raises the profile so the next conversation starts warmer. The engine is: meet people, sign businesses, keep it organized, raise the profile, repeat.

If you ever feel pulled in four directions, come back to Lane 1. Getting great Atlanta businesses on the platform is the priority. Everything else exists to make that easier.

## Your 30/60/90 ramp

This is built around your training, not against it. You are in-season now and your offseason opens July 1. The early weeks stay light. The work scales as your schedule opens. Football and recovery come first, every time.

### Days 1 to 30: Learn and start warm

Light load. The goal is to get fluent and land your first handful of listings from people you already know.

- [ ] Read the Partner Primer until the "5 things" are automatic
- [ ] Practice the 30-second and 2-minute pitches out loud with Chalece
- [ ] Make a first list of 15 to 20 Atlanta businesses you already have a connection to
- [ ] Have 3 to 5 warm conversations and get the first listings started
- [ ] Set up the master tracker with Chalece so the process exists before it gets busy

### Days 31 to 60: Build the system

Your offseason is open now, so this is where it scales. Turn warm wins into a repeatable motion.

- [ ] Grow outreach to a steady weekly rhythm that fits your training
- [ ] Get to 10 to 15 live listings with photos and stories
- [ ] Open your first real community or partnership conversation
- [ ] Represent The BLACQList at one event or gathering
- [ ] Tighten the quality check so every new Page meets the bar

### Days 61 to 90: Own the lane

You run the Atlanta seeding effort. Keep one eye on your Sept 12 trip and ease the load around it.

- [ ] Own the Atlanta pipeline toward the 150 listing target
- [ ] Lock in at least one ongoing community partnership
- [ ] Build a simple events and outreach calendar you run
- [ ] Hand Chalece a clean weekly picture of where the numbers stand
- [ ] Lighten the schedule the week of the trip and protect your recovery and your cut

## Working cadence

- **Weekly partner sync, 30 minutes.** Pick a fixed slot that dodges game days and hard training days. You bring the numbers. Chalece brings the build status. You both pick the top three things for the week.
- **What you report:** businesses contacted, businesses signed, listings live, and anything stuck.
- **Keep it light between syncs.** A shared tracker beats a stream of messages.

---

## Guardrails so this stays real

- **Training and recovery come first.** No outreach on game days. Nothing heavy the day before or after a hard session.
- **Respect the cut and the trip.** Through Sept 12 your body comp goal is real work of its own. The plan ramps around it, not over it.
- **One lane at a time when you are busy.** A busy football week means Lane 1 only, and even that stays small. Better to sign two businesses well than chase ten badly.
- **No fantasy weeks.** If a week is too full, we cut the business work, not the recovery. Say so out loud and we adjust.
