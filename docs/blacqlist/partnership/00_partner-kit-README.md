# The BLACQList Partner Activation Kit

This kit brings Ebony fully into The BLACQList as a partner. It does two jobs:

1. Gets you informed enough to talk about The BLACQList anywhere, to anyone, with confidence.
2. Defines exactly how you push the business forward using what you are already great at: logistics, in-person presence, and communication.

## The one thing to remember

The BLACQList only works if it is full of great listings. Seeding those listings and building the community around them is the single hardest part of launch. That is the exact thing you are built to solve. Your partnership is not decoration. It is the lever that de-risks the whole launch.

## What is in the kit

| File | What it is | Read it when |
|---|---|---|
| **01 Partner Primer** | Everything you need to know to speak about The BLACQList cold | First. This is your foundation. |
| **02 Role & Ramp** | Your four lanes, what you own, and a 30/60/90 plan built around your training calendar | After the primer, to see where you plug in |
| **03 Pitch & Talking Points** | Pitches, FAQs, and objection answers for every audience | Before any meeting, event, or outreach |
| **04 Partnership Structure Notes** | Honest starting points for how the partnership is structured | When you and Chalece sit down to make it official |

## How to use it

- [ ] Read **01 Partner Primer** end to end once. Then reread the "If you only remember 5 things" section until it is automatic.
- [ ] Skim **02 Role & Ramp**. Pick your first lane to lean into. The recommendation is biz dev and listing acquisition, because that is what launch needs most.
- [ ] Practice the 30-second and 2-minute pitches from **03** out loud. Say them to Chalece until they feel like yours, not a script.
- [ ] Hold **04** for a real sit-down conversation. Nothing in it is legal or financial advice. It is a list of things to decide together and a flag for when to bring in a professional.

## A note on bandwidth

You are an in-season athlete. The plan respects that. The early weeks are light and shift heavier into the offseason that starts July 1. Nothing here asks you to choose between football and the business. Training and recovery come first, always.

## A note on voice

When you talk about The BLACQList in public, keep the AI side simple. It is a strength we use to build a better product. We do not get into the technical machinery. Lead with what it does for people, not how it is built.
