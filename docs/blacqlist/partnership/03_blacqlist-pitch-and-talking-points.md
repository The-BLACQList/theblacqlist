## Objections and FAQ

Short, confident answers. Do not get defensive. Most of these are real curiosity, not attacks.

**How is this different from Yelp or Google?**
Those give you a thin listing and a star rating. We give you a full page with your story, your photos, and a clear way to book or buy. And we are built specifically to find and support Black-owned businesses, not as a filter bolted onto something else.

**How is it different from Instagram?**
Instagram is great for following along, but you cannot really search it for what you need. The BLACQList is built for discovery. Search for what you want and find a trusted option fast.

**Is it free?**
Finding businesses is free. Getting your business on is free to start. Businesses can pay to stand out as we grow, and later there is a marketplace for selling products.

**So how does it make money?**
Businesses can pay to stand out and get featured. Later, vendors sell products on the platform and we take a small share of each sale. Discovery itself stays free.

**When does it launch?**
We are building it now, Atlanta first. The work right now is lining up the businesses and the community so it is strong on day one.

**What cities?**
Atlanta is the anchor and our deepest focus, and it is built to be national from the start. More cities come as we grow.

**How do I get listed?**
That is the easy part. I can get your details and a couple of photos and start your page right now.

**Is this only for Atlanta?**
No. Atlanta is where we are going deep first, but it is national by design. We are proving it in Atlanta and taking it everywhere.

**Who is behind it?**
It is founded and built by Chalece, who has a strong background in technology and product, and I am a partner driving it on the ground here in Atlanta.

## Voice: do and do not

**Do:**
- Lead with what people get, not how it is built
- Say "Find and Be Found. Keep the dollar moving."
- Be proud and clear and useful
- Tell the truth about the stage. We are building it, and that is exciting
- Make the business owner feel seen

**Do not:**
- Oversell or overhype. The brand is confident, not loud
- Get into the technical machinery or how the smart features work. Keep that simple and high level
- Call it a directory. It is not. It is a platform of real pages
- Promise dates we have not set
- Make it sound like a filter on top of Yelp

## Words to use and words to avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Platform | Directory |
| Page or mini website | Listing |
| Find and support | Browse |
| Keep the dollar moving | Shop local, generic version |
| Building it now, Atlanta first | It is launching soon, with a hard date |
| Smart features that make it better | Technical descriptions of how it works |
