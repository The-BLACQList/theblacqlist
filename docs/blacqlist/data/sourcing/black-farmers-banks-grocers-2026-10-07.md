# Black Farmers, Banks and Grocery Stores: Sourcing List

**For:** The BLACQList founder, for outreach and future listings
**Researched:** 2026-10-07
**Cities covered:** Atlanta, Houston, Chicago, Los Angeles, Washington DC, New Orleans (the six cities with live listings)

Nothing in this file has been contacted. A confirmation pass on 2026-10-07 checked every entry again and loaded the confirmed ones on staging; see section 6. Production is not touched until the founder approves it.

---

## Summary

| List | Goal | Found | High confidence | Medium confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Black farmers near our cities | 50 | **64** | 21 | 43 |
| Black-owned banks | 10 | **15** (plus 9 more on the FDIC list) | 15 | 0 |
| Black-owned grocery stores | 10 | **10** open (plus 2 strong leads) | 3 | 7 |

**What the confidence levels mean**

- **High:** a news story, a market's vendor page, a government record or the business's own website says it is Black-owned, and there is dated activity from 2024 to 2026.
- **Medium:** the only proof of Black ownership is one directory listing (usually the Black Farmers Index, which is self-reported), or the proof of recent activity is weak (for example, Instagram dates only).

**Watch out for these links.** Some old farm websites have been taken over by spam or gambling sites. Do not link to these: westsidebeeboyz.com, soilfulcity.com, nelson-farms.com, districtmarketgrocer.com.
