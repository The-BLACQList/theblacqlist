# Ticket 148: Cue, the site assistant, made conversational and page-aware

**Phase:** V1.5 · **Priority:** P1 · **Status:** In review
**Depends on:** 147 (the site assistant), 146 (the home guide), 142 (place lookup), 138 / 128 (provider, ceiling, kill switch, audit log)
**Gates:** none in this PR. No migration, no new dependency. Still behind `FEATURE_AI_ASSISTANT`. The Privacy Policy change is posted with the merge (posted date October 30, 2026; change `PRIVACY_PROVIDERS_UPDATE.postedOnLabel` if it merges later). A notice email is GATE-COMMS and waits for the founder's word.
**Decision record (founder, 2026-10-30):**

1. **The name is Cue.** The button says "Ask Cue" on the home bar and in the corner. On a phone the bar's button is an arrow, with "Ask Cue" for screen readers.
2. **The bar reads "Tell us what you're looking for".** The visible AI tag is gone. Screen readers still hear "(answered with AI)".
3. **Example text you'd text a friend.** About 10 first-person asks ("Where can I get braids in Atlanta?", "I need a dentist near me"), one picked at random each time the page loads. The server shows the first one, so the page matches before the pick.
4. **Cue writes one opener.** One sentence, 160 characters or fewer, checked before it shows. This reverses ticket 147's rule 4 ("The AI writes short need names only").
5. **A small line inside Cue:** "Cue uses AI to match your words to our listings." with a Privacy link.
6. **Page actions, by risk.** Cue scrolls to and highlights a section of the page on its own. Anything that leaves the page or changes filters waits for a tap. Cue never fills forms, signs anyone in or touches checkout.
7. **Page-aware.** Cue knows the page you're on and helps with that page.
8. **Fixed lines read like replies** ("Here's what's closest to you.", "Cue found 2 places near you.").

---

## Why

The founder walked through 147 on 2026-10-30. The answers read like a search report, the bar said "AI" before it said what it does, and two chat logs showed real bugs: "a dentist near by" went national and called it found, "chicago is not nearby me" searched all of Chicago, a later question reused that turned-down Chicago, and "in atlanta" brought back a dentist from two asks earlier. Visitors also ask about the page they're on ("are they open now?") and the assistant couldn't see it.

## Bugs fixed

| What happened | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "a dentist near by?" listed Atlanta and Chicago as "2 found" | "near by" wasn't a near word, so the search went national and still said found | More near words (near by, close by, around here, near here, in my area). Results from every city never say found |
| Near me with nothing within 10 miles showed nothing | Only one radius was tried | Wider circles of 100 and 500 miles; the line names the city and how far it is |
| "chicago is not nearby me" gave "Places in Chicago, 88" | Any place in the words set the scope, even a turned-down one, and an empty answer became a generic "Places" need | Turned-down places never set the scope. Follow-ups reuse the last needs; generic "Places" only on a first ask about a place |
| "How do I find black owned produce?" went to Chicago | The history loop reused the turned-down Chicago | The same turned-down check runs on earlier messages |
| "in atlanta" brought back the dentist from two asks ago | The model re-added old needs | The client sends the last answer's needs (`previous`, checked again on the server). Needs come only from the newest words; a place-only or time-only follow-up reuses `previous` |
| A follow-up to a Near me answer lost "near you" | Nothing remembered Near me | The chat keeps a "used Near me" flag (never a position), and the follow-up asks the browser again |
| The need name read "black owned produce" | Identity words were kept out of keywords but not names | Names drop those words and are title-cased ("Produce") |
| "Found 88 matches." and "Ask the assistant" | Old fixed copy | Rewritten in Cue's voice |

## Scope

In:

- **Shared examples** (`lib/assistant/examples.ts`, `components/assistant/useExample.ts`). The pick happens after mount.
- **Bar and panel copy** (`components/home/AiGuide.tsx`, `components/assistant/Assistant.tsx`, `components/assistant/AssistantAnswer.tsx`): the labels, button text, the footer line, and "Keep chatting with Cue".
- **Opener** (`checkOpener` in `lib/ai/assistant.ts`). It is dropped if it has any of these:
  - a link, an email or markup;
  - a number the page doesn't already show;
  - a capitalized name that isn't one of our cities, our categories, the visitor's needs, "Cue", "The BLACQList" or the page's business;
  - a result claim ("found", "best", "top", "here are").

  It is also dropped when every need finds nothing. The prompt is `VISITOR_ASSISTANT@2`, and the output cap is 1600 characters.
- **Page awareness** (`lib/assistant/page.ts`, `lib/assistant/scroll.ts`).
  - The client sends only the path. The server refuses `//`, backslashes and anything that isn't a site path.
  - **Business page.** The server does one published-row read for its name, kind, city, rating and which parts it shows.
    - Scroll on its own: hours, location, what they offer, about, contact, reviews, photos.
    - Wait for a tap: directions, call, website (https only), More like this, and Claim this page if the page is unclaimed.
  - **Discover.** The filters come from the query string, and Cue offers a tap for "Show only open now".
  - **Pricing.** Cue scrolls to the plans on its own (the plans block has `id="plans"`). It offers taps for Compare plans and Add your business.
  - **Stories, guides and collections:** the page name.
  - **Other pages:** nothing.
  - The model picks up to 3 action codes. Codes the page doesn't offer are dropped, and every link is built on the server.
  - The highlight is a 2-second ring. With reduced motion it is a jump and focus only. On a phone the sheet closes first.
- **Answer fixes** (`lib/actions/ai/assistant.ts`, `lib/assistant/near.ts`, `lib/assistant/store.ts`, `lib/assistant/ask.ts`): see Bugs fixed.
- **Privacy Policy** (`app/(public)/privacy/PrivacyPolicy.tsx`). It now names Cue and says:
  - Cue writes a short checked reply.
  - It sees the page address and only the public facts that page already shows.
  - A Near me follow-up asks the browser again, and the location is never sent to Anthropic or saved.
- **Safety plan** (`docs/blacqlist/ai/ai-safety-and-approval-plan.md`): the section is now "Visitor assistant: Cue".

Out:

- Filling forms, signing in, checkout, or any write on the visitor's behalf.
- Saving the chat or any location on our servers.
- A second model call per ask (one call, JSON out, no tool loop).
- Turning `FEATURE_AI_ASSISTANT` on in prod (the founder's call).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given the home page with the flag on, when it loads, then the bar reads "Tell us what you're looking for", the button reads "Ask Cue" (an arrow on a phone), no AI tag shows, and the example text changes on reload.
- Given "a dentist near by?" and nothing within 10 miles, when Cue answers, then it gives the nearest city with the miles and never says found.
- Given a Near me dentist answer, when the visitor says "chicago is not near me", then Cue keeps the dentist near them and never searches Chicago.
- Given that chat, when the visitor asks "How do I find Black-owned produce?", then the need is "Produce" and the search never uses Chicago.
- Given a Produce answer, when the visitor says "in atlanta", then Cue answers Produce in Atlanta only.
- Given a model opener with a link, a number not on the page, an unknown name or a result claim, then the fixed line shows instead.
- Given a business page with hours, when the visitor asks "when do they close?", then the page scrolls to the hours with a short highlight, and Cue says so.
- Given a business page with no hours, when the visitor asks "when do they close?", then Cue says the hours aren't listed and offers Call or Website. It never jumps to an unrelated section.
- Given a Near me answer or a named city, then Cue's opener never asks where the visitor is.
- Given /discover, when the visitor asks for only open ones, then a "Show only open now" button appears and nothing changes until it's tapped.
- Given /pricing, when the visitor asks which plan fits a food truck, then the page scrolls to the plans and Cue doesn't also search for food trucks.
- Given the path `//evil.example/pricing`, then Cue offers no actions.
