## 1. How returns work in Shop

Each seller sets its own return policy. It shows on each product page and at checkout, next to that seller's items. The policy can be generous, such as 30-day returns for any reason, or narrow, such as no change-of-mind returns.

Whatever the seller's policy, Shop has a minimum that every seller must meet. We call it buyer protection.

## 2. Buyer protection: what is always refundable

You can always get a refund if your order is:

| Problem | What it means | When you can open a case |
|---|---|---|
| Not received | It never arrived | From 7 days after the expected delivery date, up to 30 days after it `[Assumption: closing date]` |
| Defective | It doesn't work, or breaks in normal use soon after it arrives | Within 30 days of delivery |
| Damaged | It arrived broken or spoiled | Within 30 days of delivery |
| Wrong item | You got a different item, size, colour or quantity | Within 30 days of delivery |
| Not as described | It is materially different from the listing or photos | Within 30 days of delivery |

`[Assumption]` The founder set 30 days for "not as described". This draft uses the same 30 days for defective, damaged and wrong item, so shoppers have one window to remember. Founder to confirm.

"Not as described" means a real difference, such as a different material, a missing part, a fake brand, or photos that showed a better item. It does not cover changing your mind, or small differences in colour from screen to screen.

Buyer protection covers Shop purchases only. It does not cover services, or anything you bought on a business's own site or in person.

## 3. Opening a case

1. Go to your order and choose "Report a problem". Guest shoppers use the link in their order email.
2. Pick the problem and tell us what happened. For damaged, defective, wrong or not as described, add photos.
3. The seller has 3 business days to respond. The seller can offer a refund, a replacement, or a return, or explain why it disagrees.
4. If you and the seller agree, we close the case.
5. If the seller does not respond in 3 business days, or you don't agree, we decide. We look at the listing, photos, tracking and messages.

While a case is open, the seller's pending transfers are frozen (Seller Agreement 6.4).

## 4. Returning the item

- For a refund under buyer protection, we may ask you to send the item back. The seller pays return shipping, usually with a prepaid label.
- For damaged perishable goods, or items worth less than return shipping, we usually don't ask for a return. `[Assumption]`
- For change-of-mind returns, the seller's own policy decides whether you can return and who pays shipping.

## 5. Refunds

- Refunds go back to the card you paid with.
- A full refund includes the item price, the shipping you paid for that seller's items, and the sales tax.
- A partial refund, for example for one item out of several, includes the matching tax. `[Needs professional review]` Whether shipping is refunded on a partial return. CPA to confirm the tax treatment of refunded shipping.
- Card refunds usually show in 5 to 10 business days.

## 6. Chargebacks

If you ask your bank to reverse a charge, the bank's process decides it, and any Shop case for that order closes. We may still help. We suggest opening a Shop case first, since it is usually faster.

## 7. What we don't cover

- Changing your mind, unless the seller's policy allows returns.
- Damage you caused, or normal wear.
- Problems reported after the windows above.
- Orders where we find abuse, such as repeated false claims or returning a different item. We may decline buyer protection and close accounts for abuse.

Nothing here limits your rights under law, such as implied warranties the seller gives under state law. `[Needs professional review]`

## 8. For sellers

- **Your return policy.** Set it in your shop settings: whether you accept change-of-mind returns, the window, who pays return shipping, and any items you don't take back, such as custom or perishable items. It must not contradict buyer protection. A policy that says "no refunds" is shown with buyer protection next to it.
- **Respond within 3 business days.** If you don't, we decide.
- **Recovery.** If we refund a shopper on your order, you owe it to us. We recover it by transfer reversal, then offset against future transfers, then an invoice (Seller Agreement 8.3). We credit back our 8% fee on the refunded amount. Stripe's processing fee is not returned. See `fees-and-payouts.md`.
- **Our fault.** If a refund was caused by our error, we bear it.
- **Strikes.** A pattern of upheld cases, such as frequent "not as described", counts toward strikes.
