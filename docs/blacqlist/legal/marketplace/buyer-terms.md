## Part 1. Purchase terms

### 1. Who you are buying from

When you buy in Shop, you buy from the seller shown on the product. The seller is the seller of record. Your contract of sale is with that seller. One checkout can include products from several sellers, so you may make several contracts at once.

BLACQList runs Shop. We take your payment as the sellers' limited agent for collecting payment. When you pay us, you have paid the seller. We handle sales tax where we are registered to collect it, and we run buyer support and buyer protection. We do not make, own, store or ship the products.

### 2. Who can buy

You must be at least 18 years old to buy in Shop, signed in or as a guest. `[Needs professional review]` The live Terms require 18 for an account (§1, §3). A guest has no account, so this needs its own rule. An alternative is to allow minors to buy with a parent or guardian's consent.

You can check out as a guest. Stripe collects your email at checkout so we can send your receipt, order updates and download links.

Shop ships to the 50 US states and the District of Columbia.

### 3. Prices, tax and payment

Prices are in US dollars. The seller sets the price and the shipping. Sales tax is worked out at checkout and shown before you pay.

Payment is processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. The charge on your card statement shows "BLACQLIST* SHOP", not the seller. `[Needs professional review]`

Your order is placed when you finish checkout and we email your confirmation. A seller may cancel an order it cannot fill, and you get a full refund.

### 4. Shipping

Each seller shows a processing time of 1 to 10 business days and its shipping rates. The seller must ship within that time. If a seller cannot ship on time, we send you a delay notice no later than the ship-by date, and you can cancel that seller's part of your order for a full refund. If that part is still not shipped 7 days after the ship-by date, we cancel and refund it for you. `[Needs professional review]` Details are in the Shipping and Delivery Policy.

### 5. Returns and buyer protection

Each seller sets its own return policy, shown on the product and at checkout. Whatever the seller's policy, you can get a refund if your order is defective, damaged, the wrong item, not received, or not as described. Details, time limits and how to open a case are in the Returns, Refunds and Buyer Protection policy.

If you open a chargeback with your bank on an order, any buyer case for that order closes, and the bank's process decides it.

### 6. Digital products

Digital products are delivered as download links. Each link lasts 15 minutes, and you can download a purchase up to 5 times. Before you pay, you agree that a digital item cannot be refunded once you download it, unless it is defective or not as described. Your licence is personal and non-transferable, unless the seller states otherwise. Details are in the Digital Products Policy.

### 7. Product information

Sellers write their product listings and are responsible for them. Physical products must be shown with real photos of the item, and you can report a photo you think breaks this rule. Ownership descriptions on BLACQList are written by each business. We do not check them.

### 8. Reporting a problem

Each product page has a link to report a product or seller, including suspected counterfeit, unsafe or stolen goods, or a photo that is not real. You can also report by phone at `{Phone number for INFORM reporting}`. See `inform-act-compliance.md`.

### 9. Your information

We give the seller your name, shipping address, email and what you ordered, so it can fill your order and help you. Sellers may not use it to market to you unless you separately say yes. See the Privacy Policy.

### 10. Services

Services, such as bookings or appointments, are not sold through the Shop cart. You arrange them with the business directly, and Terms §10 applies as it does today.

### 11. Reviews

Shop has no product reviews in this version. If we add them, we will update these terms.

## Part 2. Proposed change to Terms of Service §10

### Current text

Terms §10, "Disclaimers", third paragraph, as live on 2026-11-13:

> The BLACQList is not a party to any transaction between users and listed businesses. We are not responsible for the quality, safety, legality, or any other aspect of goods or services offered by businesses listed on the Platform. Always verify business information independently before transacting.

The same section's second list says: "We do not warrant that: ... Any goods or services offered by listed businesses meet any particular standard of quality".

### Why it must change

Once Shop takes payment, "not a party to any transaction" is no longer accurate. BLACQList collects payment as the sellers' agent, appears on the card statement, collects sales tax as marketplace facilitator, and decides buyer cases. A statement that does not match how the product works is an FTC Act §5 risk and weakens the disclaimer itself. `[Needs professional review]`

### Proposed replacement for the third paragraph

> **Purchases in Shop.** When you buy a product in Shop, the seller shown on the product is the seller of that product, and your contract of sale is with that seller. BLACQList is not the seller, and does not make, own, store or ship the products. For Shop purchases, BLACQList collects payment as the seller's limited payment collection agent, collects and pays sales tax where we are registered to do so, and provides buyer support and the buyer protection described in our Shop Purchase Terms. Those Shop Purchase Terms apply to your purchase.
>
> **Everything else.** For anything you buy or arrange with a listed business outside Shop, including services and purchases on a business's own website or in person, BLACQList is not a party to the transaction.
>
> Except for the buyer protection we describe in our Shop Purchase Terms, we are not responsible for the quality, safety, legality, or any other aspect of goods or services offered by businesses listed on the Platform. Nothing in this section limits any right you have under law that cannot be waived.

The sentence "Always verify business information independently before transacting." is dropped. `[Needs professional review]` It conflicts with the Shop copy rule against "verify" and reads oddly next to on-platform checkout. Counsel may prefer to keep a version of it for off-platform dealings: "Check business information yourself before you buy outside Shop."

The bullet "Any goods or services offered by listed businesses meet any particular standard of quality" can stay.
