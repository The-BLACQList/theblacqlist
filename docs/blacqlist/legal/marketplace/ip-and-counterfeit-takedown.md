## 1. Scope

This policy covers Shop listings, product photos and digital files that may infringe:

- copyright, under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. 512);
- trademarks, including counterfeit goods;
- other rights, such as design patents or rights of publicity.

Copyright notices follow the DMCA process in sections 3 to 5. Trademark, counterfeit and other notices follow section 6. The DMCA safe harbor covers copyright only. `[Needs professional review]`

## 3. Copyright notice

Send to the designated agent at notice@theblacqlist.com, or by mail to the address in Terms §9. A notice must include:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. the copyrighted work you say is infringed;
3. the Shop listing, photo or file, with its link;
4. your name, address, phone and email;
5. a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law;
6. a statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.

`[Assumption]` We will add a web form for notices that collects these items, alongside the email.

## 4. What we do with a valid notice

1. Remove or disable the listing, photo or file promptly. Our target is 2 business days. `[Assumption]`
2. Tell the seller, with a copy of the notice. We may remove the notifier's personal contact details if they ask, but the seller needs enough to respond.
3. Stop sales of a digital file that is the subject of the notice. Past buyers keep downloads already made. `[Needs professional review]` Whether to disable remaining downloads.
4. Record the notice against the seller for the repeat infringer policy.

Notices missing key items get a reply asking for them.

## 5. Counter-notice

A seller who believes the material was removed by mistake or misidentification can send a counter-notice to the designated agent. It must include:

1. the seller's physical or electronic signature;
2. the material removed and where it appeared;
3. a statement under penalty of perjury that the seller believes in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification;
4. the seller's name, address and phone, and a statement that the seller consents to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for its address, or if outside the US, any district where BLACQList may be found, and will accept service from the person who sent the notice.

We send the counter-notice to the person who sent the notice. We restore the material in 10 to 14 business days after receiving the counter-notice, unless the notifier tells us it has filed a court action to stop the infringement.

## 6. Trademark, counterfeit and other claims

The DMCA does not cover these. Our process:

- Rights owners send a notice to notice@theblacqlist.com with: who they are, the right and proof of it, such as a registration number, the listing, and why it infringes or is counterfeit.
- We review it and may remove the listing. For suspected counterfeits we may also cancel and refund open orders and hold related transfers.
- The seller may respond with evidence, such as an invoice from an authorised supplier or a licence. We decide whether to restore.
- Counterfeit goods are prohibited. An upheld counterfeit claim can lead to immediate suspension (Seller Agreement 21.2).

Shoppers can report suspected counterfeits using "Report this item" on each product page. See `inform-act-compliance.md`.

## 7. Repeat infringer policy

The DMCA safe harbor requires a repeat infringer policy that is reasonably carried out.

- Each valid notice that leads to removal, and is not reversed by a counter-notice, is an infringement mark against the seller.
- Each mark is also a strike under Seller Agreement section 21.
- **3 marks in any 12 months** end the seller's ability to sell in Shop, and may end the account. `[Assumption]`
- We may end selling sooner for clear or wilful infringement, or counterfeits.
- A seller removed under this policy may not reopen through another account (Terms §3).

## 8. Misuse

Knowingly false notices or counter-notices can create liability under 17 U.S.C. 512(f). We may ignore notices from people who repeatedly send false ones.
