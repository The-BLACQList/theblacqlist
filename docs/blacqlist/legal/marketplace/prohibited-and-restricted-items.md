## Why this exists

Shop takes payment through BLACQList's own Stripe account, so anything Stripe does not allow can put the whole platform's payments at risk, not just one seller's. This list is aligned with Stripe's Restricted Businesses list as the founder described it. `[Needs professional review]` Before launch, someone must check this list against Stripe's current list and BLACQList's Stripe agreement, since Stripe updates it.

This policy is part of the Seller Agreement.

You may not list or sell any of these in Shop:

| Drugs | Illegal drugs, prescription drugs, drug paraphernalia, CBD, hemp and hemp-derived products, kratom, and products marketed as legal alternatives to drugs |

## 2. Restricted items

You may sell these only if you meet the conditions and confirm it on each product when you list it. Each first listing in a restricted category goes to the founder's review queue, even after your first 3 products. `[Assumption]`

### 2.1 Food

- You meet your state's cottage-food law, or you hold the food license or permit your state requires.
- Many cottage-food laws do not allow shipping across state lines. If yours does not, you must limit shipping to your own state, and the listing form must enforce it. `[Needs professional review]` v1 shipping profiles cover the 50 states and DC. A per-product ship-to limit may be needed before food can be sold.
- Labels meet state law and, where they apply, FDA labelling rules, including ingredients and major allergens.
- Perishable food must be declared as needing cold shipping, with the packaging and shipping speed to keep it safe. You are responsible if it spoils in transit.
- No raw milk, home-canned low-acid foods, or meat and poultry that needs USDA inspection unless you hold it. `[Assumption]`

### 2.2 Cosmetics

- You meet the Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act of 2022 (MoCRA) where it applies to you, including facility registration and product listing with the FDA, unless you qualify for the small business exemption.
- Labels list ingredients and give a US address or phone number for adverse event reports.
- You keep records of safety substantiation and report serious adverse events as MoCRA requires.
- No drug claims, such as "treats acne" or "regrows hair", unless the product is a legally marketed over-the-counter drug. `[Needs professional review]` OTC drugs may need their own category or a ban.

### 2.3 Children's products

Products designed or intended mainly for children 12 or under:

- You have a Children's Product Certificate, based on testing by a CPSC-accepted third-party lab, as the Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act (CPSIA) requires.
- The product and its packaging carry permanent tracking labels.
- Small parts, choking hazard warnings, lead and phthalate limits and other CPSC rules are met.
- You must upload the certificate, or give it on request within 2 business days. `[Assumption]`
- If you import the product, you meet CPSC certificate eFiling rules. `[Needs professional review]`

### 2.4 Supplements

- No disease claims: you may not say a supplement diagnoses, treats, cures or prevents a disease.
- Structure and function claims carry the FDA disclaimer and have been notified to the FDA, as the Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act requires.
- The label meets FDA supplement labelling rules.
- `[Needs professional review]` Stripe treats some supplements and nutraceuticals as restricted. Confirm BLACQList's Stripe account allows them before this category opens.

## 3. How we enforce it

- **At listing:** the seller picks a category and confirms the conditions. Prohibited categories cannot be picked.
- **Review:** the founder reviews each seller's first 3 products and the first product in each restricted category. Later products are spot-checked.
- **Reports:** shoppers can report a product from its page. See `inform-act-compliance.md`.
- **When we find a breach:** we remove the product, may cancel and refund open orders for it, and may hold related transfers. An upheld breach is a strike. Weapons, drugs, counterfeit, stolen or recalled goods can lead to immediate suspension without strikes (Seller Agreement 21.2).
- **Ownership label plays no part.** These rules apply the same way to every seller, whatever its ownership label.
