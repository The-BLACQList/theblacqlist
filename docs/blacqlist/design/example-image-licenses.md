Plus three first-party logos (`logo-cafe.png`, `logo-pro.png`, `logo-studio.png`) that carry no third-party licence. Manifest: `lib/example/photoCredits.ts`.

**Last updated:** 2026-11-13

Every file in `public/images/example/` is used **only** on the Preview/staging sample pages under `/example/*`, which show the fictional business "Test Business 123" in three variants (café with merch, business consultancy, graphic-design studio in Atlanta). None of these files is used on production marketing pages or in any customer-facing listing. Both the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) and the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) permit commercial use without attribution; we record the photographer and photo page anyway, as `.claude/rules/3d-assets.md` requires a recorded source, author, licence name and commercial-use confirmation for every third-party asset.

Team headshots were deliberately **not** sourced from stock: no stock photo of a person comes with a model release that would cover presenting them as a named employee of a (fictional) business, so the sample pages use initials avatars instead. Photos that include people show only hands, backs or distant figures; none has a recognisable face, so every `identifiablePeople` flag is `false`.

**Source of record:** `lib/example/photoCredits.ts`. `tests/example-fixtures.test.ts` checks that the files on disk and that list match one to one; this document is the human-readable copy of the same data and must be updated in the same change as the manifest.

## Restrictions that bind us

Only what the two licence pages themselves say, as read on 2026-11-13.

### Unsplash License ([unsplash.com/license](https://unsplash.com/license))

Photos may be downloaded and used for free for commercial and non-commercial purposes, without permission or attribution. Two things are **not** permitted:

- Images cannot be sold without significant modification.
- Compiling images from Unsplash to replicate a similar or competing service.

For us that means: the example images stay inside the sample pages; we never offer them for download, resale or as a browsable image library.

### Pexels License ([pexels.com/license](https://www.pexels.com/license/))

No file in the pool currently comes from Pexels, but it is the approved fallback source, so its terms are recorded here. Photos are free to use, attribution is not required, and modification is allowed. **Not** allowed:

- Identifiable people may not appear in a bad light or in a way that is offensive.
- Selling unaltered copies of a photo or video, e.g. as a poster, print or physical product, without modifying it first.
- Implying endorsement of your product by people or brands on the imagery.
- Redistributing or selling the photos and videos on other stock photo or wallpaper platforms.
- Using the photos or videos as part of your trade-mark, design-mark, trade-name, business name or service mark.

If a Pexels file is ever added, record it in `lib/example/photoCredits.ts` with `source: 'pexels'` and `license: 'Pexels License'`, and add its row to the table above.
