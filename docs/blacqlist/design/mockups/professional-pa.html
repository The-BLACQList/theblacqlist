About Carter & Co.

Founder portrait — environmental, office context, warm grade

Danielle Carter spent eleven years running operations for two of Atlanta's fastest-growing logistics firms before opening her own practice in 2019 — with one conviction: Black-owned businesses don't need more inspiration, they need infrastructure.

“Our clients don't hire us to dream bigger. They hire us to build the systems their dream deserves.”— Danielle Carter, Founder & Principal

Today the firm works with twenty-plus companies a year across food service, wellness, and professional trades — the same corridors you'll find on The BLACQList. Engagements start with the numbers and end with an operating rhythm the team actually keeps.
