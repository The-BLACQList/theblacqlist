Map explore — three layouts on the brand-dark map

The self-hosted Protomaps base map styled as a Gold Node Network surface: near-black ground, warm-toned streets, gold pins. All options share the tier-aware pin system (below), faceted filters, a click popup that links to the business page, clustering, and — non-negotiable — a parallel accessible results list so the map is an enhancement, never the only path. 272 staging listings are already geocoded and ready to render.

Pin system: Certified Verified Claimed Unclaimed · prominence = trust, never pay — sponsored gets list priority + badge, not a bigger pin
Pick one: MP-A · MP-B · MP-C — hybrids welcome.
MP-A

Full-Bleed Explorer

The map IS the page — edge to edge under the nav. Search and filters float on top; results live in a collapsible floating drawer. Mobile: full map with a swipe-up bottom sheet.

MP-B

Split Index

The proven two-pane pattern: results list left (the same D-1 cards as Discover), map right. Hovering a card highlights its pin; clicking a pin scrolls to its card. Mobile: toggle between full list and full map.

Pick one: MP-A · MP-B · MP-C (hybrids welcome — e.g. "B on desktop, A's bottom sheet on mobile"). Common to all: brand-dark self-hosted Protomaps tiles (no vendor, no keys), tier-aware pins (prominence = trust, never pay), clustering, faceted filters, popup → business page, reduced-motion (no fly-to animations), and a complete accessible results list. Tier ladder on the map: unclaimed = dot · claimed = full popup · verified/certified = photo popup + brighter pin · paid presentation extras come later with Product, tied to pricing.
