The story

About Onyx Frame

Onyx Frame is a two-person studio out of Bronzeville — Jordan on stills, Simone on motion. They shoot the businesses, families, and rooms of the South Side the way they deserve to be seen: unhurried, dignified, in their own light.

“We're not documenting a moment. We're building an archive this neighborhood owns.”— Jordan Hayes, Co-founder

The studio's restaurant and founder work appears across The BLACQList — much of the documentary photography direction this platform uses started in conversations with studios like this one.
